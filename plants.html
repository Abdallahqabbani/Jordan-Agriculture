Plant Diseases

Common plant diseases that affect agricultural crops, including bacterial, viral, fungal, and pest-related conditions.

Fruit Plants Vegetable
Bacterial

Bacterial Infections

Diseases caused by pathogenic bacteria that infect plant tissues, often leading to wilting, spots, and tissue decay.

Example:
Fire blight in fruit trees and bacterial leaf spot in various crops are common bacterial diseases.
Fungal

Fungal Infections

Diseases caused by fungi that create spores and spread through air, water, or soil contact, affecting plant health.

Example:
Powdery mildew, rust diseases, and root rot are prevalent fungal conditions in many crops.
Viral

Viral Infections

Diseases caused by plant viruses transmitted through insects, contaminated tools, or infected plant material.

Example:
Tobacco mosaic virus and cucumber mosaic virus affect a wide range of agricultural plants.
